How Therapist Nook works

From the first intake form to the invoice, in one workspace.

Intake
A patient fills your intake form

Share one public link. The record creates itself — name, contact and referral source in place before the first session.

Schedule
Book it once, both calendars agree

Two-way Google Calendar sync, with the Meet link already in the invite. Nothing double-booked, nothing retyped.

Assess
Full inventories, scored automatically

Send a link. Gender-specific norms, T-scores and subscales come back in seconds — no manual lookup tables.

Understand
The clinical picture, drawn from the record

Upload the session transcript and get a summary, treatment goals and open questions — sourced only from this patient's approved record.

Get paid
A month of sessions, one invoice

Select the sessions, generate a combined invoice, and see what's outstanding without opening a spreadsheet.
