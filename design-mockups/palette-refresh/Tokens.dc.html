Color System — Dusk Lavender (v4)

After the Behance EHR reference (HealthPlus), the “quiet grey card” direction from v3 is gone. Cards that were flat neutral grey now carry a genuinely visible lavender tint, and “online” gave up its separate steel-blue hue to become solid lavender too — the product now runs on exactly two hues: lavender for everything routine, and one deep berry rose reserved for the single alarm state (overdue / pending / absent). Page ground stays pure white; color lives on cards and chips, never the page.

Reference: the HealthPlus EHR project on Behance. Its cards separate from a near-all-white page via a genuinely tinted fill or a solid brand-color “hero” card — never a grey that reads as absence of color. That's the move borrowed here: replace grey fills with visible lavender, and fold the third status hue (steel blue) into lavender so the whole app reads as one consistent color family.

Card fill — grey retired, real lavender in

The single biggest change in v4. Recent-patient rows, patient-list rows, and “received” payment cards all used a near-invisible violet-grey (#F5F3FA) that read as plain grey at a glance. They now use a new token, --primary-tint, saturated enough to actually register as lavender while staying light enough for a full-row fill.
